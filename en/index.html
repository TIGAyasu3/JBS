WHY JBS

Hiring mismatches start
where there is no standard.

01

Every institution has its own bar

Curricula and completion criteria differ from one LPK or school to the next. Two "graduates" are not comparable.

02

Paperwork doesn't show readiness

A language certificate says nothing about workplace rules, reporting habits or preparation for daily life in Japan.

03

Gaps surface after hiring

When expectations diverge after arrival, the cost lands on the employer and the candidate alike.

THE STANDARDS

Four domains of readiness
for working in Japan.

Assessment criteria and pass marks are published and applied the same way to every candidate, whichever institution trained them.

JAPANESE

Japanese language

Understanding instructions, reporting and consulting, and everyday conversation at work.

WORKPLACE

Workplace readiness

Punctuality, rules, ho-ren-so reporting habits and working in a Japanese team.

JOB SKILLS

Job skills

Core knowledge and practical skills for the target role, assessed against role-specific criteria.

LIFE

Life readiness

Housing, administrative procedures and daily habits needed to settle in Japan.

HOW IT WORKS

Training and assessment kept apart, then connected to employers.

1
Training institution (LPK / school)

Training

Each institution delivers Japanese language and work-preparation training.

2
JBS

Assessment

Four domains assessed with published criteria and a standardised method.

3
JBS

Certificate

After Advisory Board approval, a report with domain levels is issued.

4
Employer in Japan

Review and hire

Employers compare candidates on the results and move to interview and hiring.

FOR EMPLOYERS IN JAPAN

Get the evidence
before the interview.

With JBS results, candidates from different institutions sit on the same scale. Interviews can focus on what an assessment can't show.

  • One scale across candidatesDomain levels show strengths and gaps at a glance.
  • A Japanese-law contracting partnerEnquiries and contracts are handled by our Japanese entity, in Japanese.
  • Candidates from many institutionsGraduates of every LPK using JBS are introduced on equal terms.
  • Standards refined by retentionPost-placement outcomes are reviewed yearly and fed back into the criteria.
